GPS Map Photo Privacy Policy

No Data Collected 100% Local Storage No Remote Servers
Effective Date: January 1, 2025  |  Developer: AKM

Thank you for using GPS Map Photo ("the Application"). Your privacy is extremely important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how the Application handles your information, requests device permissions, and ensures your photos and data remain completely safe and private.

🛡️ Zero Data Collection Guarantee: GPS Map Photo does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share your personal photos, camera captures, or precise location coordinates on any external or remote servers. All image processing, map stamp generation, and photo saving occur exclusively locally on your physical mobile device.

1. Device Permissions & Usage

To provide its core functionality of capturing photos with live GPS location and map stamp overlays, GPS Map Photo requests the following specific permissions on your Android device:

  • Camera Permission (CAMERA)

    Used strictly locally on your device to capture real-time photos so that GPS map stamps, timestamps, and address overlays can be applied. Camera feeds and captured photos are never uploaded, streamed, or saved anywhere off your device.

  • Location Permission (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION)

    Used strictly on-device to retrieve your current GPS coordinates, altitude, plus code, and address for overlaying onto your captured photos. Your location data is processed locally in real time and is never tracked, stored externally, or shared with third parties.

  • Storage & Media Access (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE)

    Used to save your final stamped photos directly to your device's local gallery and media storage, as well as to allow you to select existing photos for viewing.

  • Internet & Network Access (INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE)

    Required to fetch map tile imagery from Google Maps SDK, retrieve address geocoding and basic weather data for the stamp overlay, and load advertisements served by Google AdMob.

2. Data Storage & Security

All application operational data—including your custom stamp template settings, preferences, and saved photos—is stored locally in private application storage on your device. Uninstalling the Application will automatically remove this local app data.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertisements

GPS Map Photo integrates official Google Play Services to provide map previews and display advertisements. These third-party services may collect device identifiers (such as Advertising ID) in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob Policies & Restrictions

4. Children's Privacy

The Application does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
