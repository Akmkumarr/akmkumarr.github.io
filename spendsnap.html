SpendSnap Privacy Policy

com.akm.spendsnap

Effective Date: May 24, 2024 • Version 1.0

✓ 100% Offline App ✓ Zero Cloud Servers ✓ Local Encrypted Database ✓ On-Device Processing

1 Introduction & 100% Offline Commitment

Welcome to SpendSnap (package identifier com.akm.spendsnap). SpendSnap is a privacy-first, 100% offline personal finance and expense management application designed specifically for Android devices.

We believe that your personal financial transactions, income details, budget targets, and expense metrics belong strictly to you. SpendSnap is engineered from the ground up to operate completely without internet connection requirements or cloud synchronization. Your financial data never leaves your personal device under any circumstances.

🔒 Core Privacy Guarantee: SpendSnap does not send, upload, transmit, or broadcast any of your transaction records or personal information to external servers or third parties.

2 Zero Cloud Data Transmission

SpendSnap operates entirely self-contained on your mobile device. To ensure complete user transparency:

  • No Remote Backend Servers: We do not maintain, operate, or connect to any remote servers, cloud databases, or web API endpoints.
  • No Telemetry or Analytics: We do not embed third-party analytics SDKs, crash reporting tools, behavioral trackers, or advertising frameworks (such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or Mixpanel).
  • No Account Registration: SpendSnap does not require user registration, email sign-in, OAuth authentication, or personal identity verification to function.

3 Device Permissions & Usage

SpendSnap requests optional device permissions strictly to enable key automated expense tracking and report generation features. All data accessed through these permissions is processed locally on-device.

Notification Access NotificationListenerService

Used exclusively for automated transaction parsing. When enabled, SpendSnap reads incoming SMS/App notification alerts from your banking and payment apps (e.g., UPI, debit/credit card alerts) to extract transaction amounts, merchant names, and categories automatically.

Note: Notification text is parsed instantaneously in memory on your device and is never stored as raw notification text or shared externally.

Local Storage & Storage Access Framework SAF / Read & Write Storage

Used only when you explicitly trigger data export or import operations. This allows SpendSnap to read backup CSV/PDF files you choose to import or save generated CSV expense sheets and PDF financial reports directly to your device's local download or document directories.

Biometric Authentication BiometricPrompt API

Used to provide local app lock capability (Fingerprint / Face Unlock). Biometric credentials are authenticated directly by the Android OS hardware security module (KeyStore). SpendSnap never collects, views, or stores biometric data.

4 Data Storage & Security

All your financial entries, categories, budgets, and application preferences are stored in a local SQLite database located within SpendSnap's isolated, sandboxed private application storage space on your device.

  • On-Device Encryption: Database files are protected by standard Android OS sandbox security and hardware-backed storage encryption.
  • Data Retention & Control: You retain 100% control over your data. You can export your data at any time or reset application data directly from app settings.
  • Permanent Deletion: Uninstalling SpendSnap from your Android device permanently and irreversibly deletes all stored transaction history and local database files.

5 Children's Privacy & Policy Updates

Children's Privacy: SpendSnap does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since SpendSnap collects zero personal information from any user, it fully complies with COPPA and global child protection standards.

Policy Updates: We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect app feature enhancements. Any revisions will be published at this official URL with an updated "Effective Date". Because SpendSnap does not collect user contact information, users are encouraged to review this page periodically.
